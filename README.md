# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes transactional retail sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, discount impact, regional performance, customer segments, and product-level performance.

The analysis was performed using **MySQL/SQL** and visualized through an interactive **Power BI dashboard**.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, quantity, and profit margin
- Compare performance across categories and sub-categories
- Identify high-profit and loss-making products
- Analyze regional and customer-segment performance
- Understand the impact of discounts on profitability
- Identify areas where profitability can be improved
- Present business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Category Performance

Technology generated the strongest overall profit performance.

Furniture generated substantial sales but significantly lower profitability, showing that high sales volume does not always result in strong profit.

### Regional Performance

The West region generated the highest profit among the regions analyzed.

The Central region showed comparatively lower profitability and provides an opportunity for further investigation.

### Discount Impact

Higher discounts can negatively affect profitability for certain products.

The Tables sub-category showed particularly weak profitability at higher discount levels.

### Product Performance

The dashboard identifies both highly profitable products and products generating significant losses.

This helps identify products that should be promoted, reviewed, or managed more carefully.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Review low-margin products, particularly within Furniture.
2. Control high discount levels where they reduce profitability.
3. Focus on products that consistently generate strong profit.
4. Investigate the lower profitability of the Central region.
5. Monitor consistently loss-making products and customers.
6. Use customer profitability analysis to support business decisions.
